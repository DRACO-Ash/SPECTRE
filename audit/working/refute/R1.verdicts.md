## Notes

**Systemic findings**

● **Web layer.** The web layer turns every heuristic score into miss_distance_km and stamps it "Computed via spectre.astro ... solver". F-S02-07 to F-S02-12 and F-S01-16 are one root cause with several panels. A single typed score or provenance field fixes them together.

● **Burn-vector construction.** Burn vectors in the maneuvers.py wrappers are built ad hoc: from v_hat at departure, from the target's state at arrival, or from red's unmanoeuvred orbit. That approach produced F-S01-06, F-S01-07, F-S01-10, F-S02-06 and the hop parity issues below. A shared "fly the transfer and take v_after − v_before" helper would remove the class.

● **New item, outside the candidate list: V-bar wrapper burn 2 sign.** vbar_hop_intercept burn 2 is −b_hat (maneuvers.py:503). Under CW, ẋ at n_hops·T/2 is (−1)^n_hops·ẋ0, so for the default n_hops = 3 the exported burn 2 doubles the radial velocity instead of nulling it. Executed: residual ẋ −10.975 m/s for 3 hops, 0.000 for 2 hops. The threat sweep uses n_hops = 3 (threat.py:634). This is the mirror image of F-S01-08: the sibling the reviewer treated as correct is the one that is wrong at the default.

● **H-bar parity residual (from F-S01-08).** hbar_hop_intercept burn 2 is correct for odd n_hops and doubles ż for even n_hops. Even counts are reachable via coast hours (maneuver.py:1253-1262). Severity Low.

● **Many solvers are reachable only through the "coast hours as hop count" overload** (maneuver.py:1253). Changing coast time changes the hop count and the parity silently.
