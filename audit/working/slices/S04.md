Lens:        L11 numerical and scientific correctness
Severity:    High
Confidence:  High
Epistemics:  fact
Location:    spectre/astro/monte_carlo.py:449-451
Evidence:
        t_span_s = horizon_hours * 3600.0 + epoch_offset_s
        if t_span_s <= 0:
            t_span_s = 60.0
Issue:       The burn-timing perturbation is applied to the end of the propagation span rather than to the burn instant: every sample still burns at t = 0 from the same pre-state, and each is then evaluated at a different final epoch, while the `pre_epoch` argument (monte_carlo.py:424, vulture 100% unused) that would anchor a timing shift is never read.

## D. Notes
● Mean versus osculating (inference): the Gauss inversion is an osculating relation applied to SGP4 mean elements. For a single impulse the first-order change in mean and osculating elements agrees to O(J2·Δa), so the approach is acceptable; SMA is derived from `no_kozai` (spectre/astro/pattern_of_life.py:363-365) rather than the Brouwer mean motion SGP4 propagates with. The offset is common to both sides and cancels in Δa to first order. Not a finding; a human should confirm the convention is intended.
● Hand cases worked: n·Δa/2 for Δa = 12.66 km at a = 6914.3 km gives 6.95 m/s (tests expect 6.95); 2·v·sin(0.05°) gives 13.25 m/s; Hohmann overshoot +1.1% and +10.9% reproduced; RAAN rate −4.49°/day at 550 km, 53°; Rozenberg airmass 40 at the horizon; Welch two-tailed p = I_{ν/(ν+t²)}(ν/2, 1/2) is the correct identity and the continued fraction matches Numerical Recipes betacf.
● RNG: `default_rng(random_seed)` with default 42, and `hypothesis_from_tle_record` never sets a seed, so every operator run uses seed 42. Reproducible but not varied; the seed is shown in the footer.
● Integrator: RK45, rtol 1e-8, atol 1e-10 on mixed km and km/s states is adequate for 48 h (inference). The pre-state is SGP4 TEME treated as inertial, acceptable over 48 h. `horizon_h` from the form is not clamped in spectre/web/routes/pol.py:762 (outside the slice, for S-web reviewer).
● spectre/web/routes/pol.py:799 sets `hypothesis.prediction_horizon_hours` with a `type: ignore`; the attribute does not exist on the dataclass and is never read (outside the slice).
● Regime probabilities at n = 500 have a standard error up to 2.2 percentage points; the template shows them to 0.1% (pol_mc_results.html:33). Included in F-S04-10/-12 spirit; not raised separately.
